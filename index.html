Privacy Policy

LLB’s Empire · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the LLB’s Empire mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The app is operated by LLB’s Empire, Madhuravoyal, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

By creating an account and using the app, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not create an account.

What the app does

LLB’s Empire is a real estate advisory app for Chennai. It lets you browse houses, villas, CMDA-approved plots and commercial spaces, view locality maps, and contact our team for advice. To use the app, you create an account so that we know who is using it and can follow up on your interest.

Information we collect

Account information you give us when you sign up:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • District where you are located
  • A password. Your password is handled by our sign-in service and stored in an encrypted (hashed) form. We cannot see or read it.

Information you give us when you contact us. If you send an enquiry, or call or message us through the app, we receive the details you provide, such as the property you are interested in and your message.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device identifiers and notification tokens. If you allow notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging, which creates an identifier for your device so we can send you notifications.
  • Diagnostic data. We may collect basic information about crashes and errors, such as app version, device model and Android version, to keep the app working well.
  • Sign-in session. To keep you signed in, the app stores a secure sign-in token on your device until you sign out.

We do not collect your precise location. We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or SMS messages.

Why we collect it and how we use it

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To know who is using the app, so we can respond to your enquiries and give property advice by phone, email or message.
  • To understand which districts and areas our users come from, so we can improve the properties and services we show.
  • To send notifications about properties and updates, if you have allowed them.
  • To help you recover your account (for example, a password reset email).
  • To fix problems, improve the app and protect it and our users from misuse.

We use your information only for the purposes above. We do not sell your personal information.

In short: we collect your name, phone, email and district so that we can run your account and contact you about properties. We do not sell your data.

Where your information is stored

Your account details are stored securely with Supabase, a database and sign-in service provider, on secure cloud servers. Access to the stored data is restricted. Each user can read and change only their own account details inside the app, and only authorised people on our team can view the user list.

Sharing of information

We share information only with the service providers listed above who help us run the app, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and safety. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

Data security

Data sent between the app and our services is protected in transit using HTTPS. Your sign-in token is stored in your device’s secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep information

We keep your account details for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and profile details from our database. We may keep limited records for a short time where the law requires it or to settle a dispute. Notification identifiers are kept until you uninstall the app or turn off notifications.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your name, phone number and district in the app’s profile section. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for corrections.
  • Delete your account and data: see the section below.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Sign out: you stay signed in on your device until you tap Sign Out in the app.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: open the app, go to your Profile, and tap Delete Account. Your account and the details linked to it (name, phone number, email and district) will be deleted.

Without the app: if you can no longer open the app, email us at plantgreeninertiamanagement@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top when we do. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
